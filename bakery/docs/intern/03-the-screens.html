Recipes, and things you can ignore at first

Products, dough types, formulas, and ingredients are the catalog the orders point at. You can open Formulas and Products to see that a “concha” on an order is a real recipe in grams. Do not edit them.

Some old pages are still in the software under Historical Navigation. They are there so nothing from the past disappears. They are not the daily menu. If a page name says invoice and it is not Billing Center, leave it. Old invoice generators redirect to Billing Center so the bakery cannot accidentally bill from two different systems.

Counter pickup slips, the customer portal, and the SF Baker classes (courses, a baking journal, a guided week) live in this same codebase. They are real products. They are not the wholesale Thursday you just walked. Learn the Thursday first.

How to tell the pages apart when they overlap

Several screens mention routes. They are not substitutes for each other.

  • Standing Orders is the weekly habit.
  • Daily Orders is the promise for one date.
  • Driver Assignment is who drives which stops that date.
  • My Route is the driver doing the stops.
  • Route Closeout is the van math after the route.
  • Daily Run is the manager’s checklist that the date can close.
  • Bakery Manager is the phone view that sends you to those pages. It does not replace them.
