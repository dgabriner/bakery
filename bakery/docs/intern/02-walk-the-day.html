Walk a day

Follow Thursday, October 1, 2026, from the photo of the route back to the orders, then forward to the bake list and the bill. Sign in first. Stay on Staging. Look, and do not save.

On the office copy of the bakery’s data, that Thursday had 51 orders, 25 stops placed on routes, and 16 deliveries confirmed in the software. Van loads were not recorded, and the production plan was not committed. You are supposed to notice those gaps. A tidy demo would hide them.

Staging is its own copy. If a number on the page is different, the page is the one to believe. The links below were checked only as far as the sign-in page. They are the real Staging addresses, and they ask for your code before they show the day.

Use the administrator code. A driver code will bounce you to My Route and hide the rest.

1. See the day as photos

Start here because this page only shows the day. It is the easiest picture of “the software was used.”

Route Summary for October 1. The first time you open a link with locale=en, the site switches to English and remembers.

Open Route Summary

https://staging.sourflour.org/route_summary.php?date=2026-10-01&locale=en

Check the date on the page. It should be Thursday, October 1, 2026. You should see stops with a customer, an amount, and a driver. Some stops have a photo. Some do not.

Read three stops. Notice which ones look delivered and which ones do not. Do not edit anything.

2. See who was assigned the stops

Driver Assignment is where a manager builds that day’s routes.

Open Driver Assignment

https://staging.sourflour.org/driver_assignment.php?date=2026-10-01&locale=en

Look for drivers, and for orders that have no driver. An unassigned order is still an order. Removing it from a route must not delete the store’s bread.

Do not drag stops. Dragging saves a new route. Do not press a build or save button.

3. Open the screen the driver uses

My Route. This link asks you to choose a driver, then shows that driver’s October 1 stops.

Open My Route

https://staging.sourflour.org/driver.php?date=2026-10-01&change_driver=1&locale=en

Pick any driver. You should see a list of stops, and on a phone a map of the ones still ahead. This is the reference screen in this project: one job, the next stop, then a confirmation.

Open one stop that already says delivered. Read the pieces, anything taken back, and the total. That total was frozen when the driver confirmed. Later catalog prices do not change it.

Do not press confirm, skip, or save. Do not reorder the remaining stops.

4. Open the screen the manager uses on the phone

Bakery Manager. Make the browser window narrow, or open this on your phone after you are signed in.

Open Bakery Manager

https://staging.sourflour.org/manager.php?date=2026-10-01&locale=en

You should see four areas: Today, Routes, Kitchen, and Missed. This page answers “what needs a person right now?” It points at the other tools. It is not a second place to rewrite the order.

Read the page as a manager would at midmorning. What looks unfinished? What would you tap next if you were allowed to?

Open Daily Brief

6. The checklist for that date

This page writes. Opening Daily Run fills in any missing orders for the coming week. It keeps edits that are already there. Do not press Generate, Close the day, or Reopen.

Daily Run is the spine of the date. The stages run from confirming demand through production, packing, routes, delivery, billing, and closing the day.

Open Daily Run

https://staging.sourflour.org/daily_run.php?date=2026-10-01&locale=en

Walk the stages with your eyes. Notice which ones look done and which ones are open. A past date can still look unfinished. That is information, not a button you need to press.

7. The orders for that Thursday

This page writes in the same way. Opening Daily Orders can also fill the coming week. Do not type in a quantity. Do not press Generate.

Daily Orders is the commercial fact for the date.

Open Daily Orders

https://staging.sourflour.org/daily_orders.php?date=2026-10-01&locale=en

Find one store and compare the usual amount with that day’s amount. If they differ, the day’s amount is what October 1 means for that store. The other stores are unaffected. That is “dated beats standing, per customer,” on a real row.

Open Route Closeout

Billing Center for October 1. Bills come from the delivery record. Amounts here are not retyped from today’s price list.
