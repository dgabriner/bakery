What you get

A journal that makes you better, and bakers who answer

The strongest bakers are not the ones with secret recipes. They are the ones who record what they did and can see what changed. SF Baker membership is built around exactly that habit.

Your baking journal

Log starters, formulas, and batches with temperatures, timing, and photos of the crumb. Patterns emerge quickly when each loaf has a record.

The 1,000-loaf path

Every logged bake counts toward your thousand. The journey view shows how far you have come — milestone by milestone, not perfection by perfection.

Sour Flour Hotline access

Stuck on a stubborn dough? SF Bakers reach the bakery's own bakers for troubleshooting — the same people who read this zone's advice into practice at scale.

How it works

From zero to first logged loaf in ten minutes

  1. Create your account.
    Your mobile number and a unique 4-digit code — no email required, no spam, ever. Open the signup →
  2. Name your starter.
    Give your culture a home in the journal: flour blend, feeding ratio, and the room it lives in.
  3. Log your first batch.
    Formula, dough temperature, bulk time, how it shaped, how it baked. Imperfect notes beat perfect silence.
  4. Bake again, compare, call if stuck.
    Change one variable at a time. When the crumb confuses you, the Hotline is a message away.

Why we built it

The learning zone is the map; the journal is the territory

Observation is the skill

Everything in our learning zone teaches you to read signs instead of clocks. The journal trains exactly that muscle: each entry asks what you saw, not just what you did.

Community without noise

We studied long-running bread communities while building this. The lesson that stuck: useful posts pair a formula with process notes and photos. Your journal entries are already halfway to a great question.

Wholesale SF Bakers: cafe and market partners track production starters, formulas, and batches in the same portal — one shared language from home kitchen to wholesale rack.
