Bread education / Sourdough / Revive a starter

Back from the back of the fridge

Three months of neglect? Usually survivable.

Starters are harder to kill than folklore claims. Grey liquid, sharp smell, separated layers — all normal hunger signals. The only true death sentence is visible mold, and even then the trash is the only casualty.

Triage first

Rescue or compost — one look

You seeVerdict
Grey-brown liquid (hooch), sharp smell, separated layersRescue. Classic starvation, not damage.
Dry dark crust on glass above the liquidRescue. Ignore the crust entirely.
Fuzzy spots: blue, green, pink, orangeCompost. Mold roots deeper than the surface shows.

The revival plan

Small, warm, patient — three to five days

  1. Keep only 15–25 g.
    Small volume = fresh food dominates fast.
  2. Feed 1:1:1 warm.
    Lukewarm water + half whole-grain flour; 75–78°F spot.
  3. Daily rhythm.
    Bubbles ~day 2, rise ~day 3. Discard down to 25 g at every feed.
  4. Graduate.
    Doubling ≤8 h + float test → scale up over two feeds → bake (first loaf).
