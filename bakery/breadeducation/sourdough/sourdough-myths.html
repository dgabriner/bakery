Lore vs. jar

Five myths your starter would like to address.

Sourdough attracts folklore the way flour attracts humidity. Each of these is half-true at best — and each retirement makes baking easier.

The docket

Myth → reality

The mythThe reality
"Add pineapple juice or grapes to start a culture."Flour + water already carry all the microbes needed; fruit just adds sugar and theater (day one).
"Miss a feeding and it's dead."Hunger slows culture; it rarely kills. Hooch is a note on the fridge, not an obituary (revival).
"Only San Francisco air makes real sourdough."Famous local strains exist, but flavor comes from YOUR flour/temp/feed history. Ours tastes like our bench, not our zip code.
"Rye starters are a different species."Rye feeds wake any culture faster (nutrients), no new life form required.
"Hooch means danger."Hooch means hungry. Pour/stir it in and feed more often.

The takeaway

What jars actually need

Food on a rhythm

Equal-weight feeds that fit YOUR schedule — fridges relax everything.

Warmth when you want speed

75–78°F for peak performance; cold for patience.
