Bread education / Troubleshooting / No oven spring

Symptom · flat, burst-anywhere bakes

Oven spring needs gas, strength, an open door, and rain.

Spring fails when any one link breaks: enough gas saved up, gluten strong enough to hold it, scoring aimed well, and real steam keeping the crust elastic. Three checks find your broken link.

Before you ask

Spring questions

What exactly is oven spring?

The rapid final rise in the first 10–20 minutes of baking, when expanding gas meets a still-stretchy crust. Thirty percent height gains are normal.

Can over-proofing kill oven spring?

Yes — spent dough has no gas left to grow. Loaves flattening during an overnight chill mean the counter proof ran long.

Does scoring depth really matter that much?

It aims the expansion. Shallow seals shut; deep deflates. Quarter inch, shallow angle.

My spring got worse when I changed flour. Why?

Weaker gluten leaks gas instead of holding it. Return to bread flour until consistent, then blend gradually.
