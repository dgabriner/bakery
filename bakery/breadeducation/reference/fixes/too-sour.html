Bread education / Troubleshooting / Too sour — or not sour enough

Symptom · acid out of balance

Sourness is a dial with three settings.

Yeast make the rise; bacteria make the bite. Whichever way your loaf missed, you steer with temperature, clock, and how hungry your starter was at mixing.

The levers

Steer either direction

LeverLess sourMore sour
Bulk temperatureWarmer (76–78°F): yeast race aheadCooler (68–70°F): bacteria catch up
Total timeShorter bulk; bake same dayLonger bulk + cold retard
Starter stateMix at peak, freshly fedMix after it falls; feed less often

Before you ask

Tang questions

Why does cold retarding make bread more sour?

In the cold, yeast mostly naps while acid-producing bacteria keep working, so acids accumulate without much rise. Shorten the chill or warm-proof longer first.

How do I make my sourdough MORE sour?

More shift time for the acid crew: cooler longer bulk, overnight retard, starter kept slightly hungry between bakes.

Does feeding frequency change sourness?

Yes — peak-fed starter is mild; tired starter imports sharp acids. Bake from peak for gentle, from fallen for bold.

Is whole grain flour souring my bread?

Usually — more nutrients and microbes accelerate acids. Blend up gradually and track tang alongside rise speed (whole grain page).
