Bread education / Troubleshooting / Dense loaf

Symptom · brick with a crust

Density is stored-up energy that never arrived.

A light loaf needs gas made, gas held, and heat that sets it in time. Four checks trace exactly where your loaf lost its lift.

Before you ask

Density questions

How strong should my starter be?

Reliably doubling in 4–8 hours and floating at peak. Creepers make bread — dense bread.

Can over-proofing make it dense too?

Yes: past-peak dough collapses in the oven. Dense-and-flat = too late; dense-but-split = too early. Walk the poke on overproofed vs underproofed.

Does whole wheat always bake denser?

Trending yes at high percentages — bran cuts gluten and drinks water. Climb the blend ladder gradually.

Fine top, tight bottom third?

Under-shaping let bubbles merge low. Firmer final shape distributes gas evenly.
