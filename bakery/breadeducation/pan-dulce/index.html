The science

What enrichment actually does

Sugar, butter, eggs, and milk transform bread's structure and its clock. Fat coats gluten strands so they slip into tenderness instead of chew; sugar ties up water and slows yeast; milk proteins brown faster and softer.

Tender, not chewy

Fat shortens gluten strands - the same chemistry behind "shortcrust." Expect cake-adjacent crumb beside your sourdough loaves.

Slower clocks

Sweet dough proofs noticeably longer than lean dough at the same temperature. Judge by puff and poke, never by the wall clock - see fermentation cues.

Browner sooner

Milk sugars and extra surface sugar race through browning. Bake until golden-bottomed, not dark-topped, or pull early and trust carryover.

Knead 12–15 minutes to a smooth, supple windowpane - buttered hands beat sticky frustration. Bulk warm until visibly puffed (about 60–90 minutes), then the family splits. This card is a classic home-kitchen method in the Sour Flour teaching style; production formulas stay at the bakery.
