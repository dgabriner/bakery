Bread education / Pan dulce family / Conchas

The icon of the case

A cookie riding a cloud.

A concha is two pastries in one: soft enriched bread underneath, sweet crackled cookie shell above. Master the mismatch - dough that rises under a cap that resists - and you have mastered the most famous pan dulce of all.

The science

Why the shell cracks

The topping is a cookie: powdered sugar, butter, flour. As the proofing dough inflates beneath it, the drying cap cannot stretch, so it tears along your score lines into ridges. Thinner caps crack wider; thicker ones stay smooth. That single relationship explains every concha photo you have ever argued about.

    Tight rounds on lined tray, spaced generously.
  3. Cap.
    40 g topping discs pressed thin between parchment; damp ball tops help them grip.
  4. Score.
    5–6 curved cuts through the cap, barely kissing dough.
  5. Proof 45–60 min.
    Until inflated; topping stretched into wide panels.
  6. Bake 350°F, 16–20 min.
    Golden bottoms tell the truth. Cool on racks.

Troubleshooting lens

When shells misbehave

Topping slides off

Dough surface too wet or proofed before capping. Cap firm, freshly rounded balls; water brush is a mist, not a wash.

No crack pattern

Cap rolled too thick, or proof too short - no expansion means no tear. Thin the paste, extend the proof.

Pale tops, raw bottoms

Oven running cool; verify with a thermometer (kit list). Bake on a lower rack briefly to set bases.

Before you ask

Concha questions

Why do concha shells crack?

The cookie topping dries while the enriched dough keeps expanding underneath; the mismatch tears it into the classic ridges. Thinner topping spreads cracks wider; thicker stays smooth.

Can I make chocolate conchas?

Yes — split the topping paste and whisk 10 g cocoa into half. Everything else stays identical; many bakeries call the two colors yellow and chocolate.

How do I score the shell pattern?

Use a lame, toothpick, or bench scraper to draw 5–6 curved lines after laying the topping disc. Cut through topping barely kissing dough; direction does not matter, consistency does.

Do I need a stand mixer?

No. Enriched dough rewards a long hand-knead on a lightly buttered counter — about 12–15 minutes to windowpane. Butter hands beat stuck dough every time.
