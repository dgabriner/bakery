Bread education / Pan dulce family / Empanadas de fruta

Half-moons of jam

The pocket that travels anywhere.

Sweet empanadas are the practical genius of the pan dulce case: a short, tender pastry folded over thick fruit filling — pineapple, apple-cinnamon, pumpkin reduced with piloncillo — sealed with a rope crimp your thumb learns once.

The recipe card

Shortcrust + filling, in grams

Dough (shortcrust style)Filling rules
Flour 400 g · cold butter 180 g · sugar 60 g · egg yolk 1 · cold milk ~120 ml · salt 5 g · baking powder 5 gJam or fruit cooked THICK (it loosens in the oven) · 2 tbsp max per 12 cm round · 1.5 cm clean border
  1. Cut butter in.
    Pea-size crumbs; work fast so butter stays solid.
  2. Bring together.
    Yolk + just enough milk till it holds; knead minimally. Chill 30 min.
  3. Roll 3 mm, cut 12 cm rounds.
    Re-roll scraps once only.
  4. Fill, fold, seal.
    Two tablespoons filling off-center; fold, press edge flat, then fork-crimp or rope-fold with one knuckle.
  5. Vent, wash, bake.
    One steam slit each; egg wash; 375°F for 20–25 min till golden.
  6. Glaze warm-ish.
    After 10 min cooling, drizzle powdered-sugar-and-milk glaze.

Before you ask

Empanada questions

Why did my empanadas leak?

Overfilled rounds, thin edges, or runny filling. Cook jams thicker than you think — they loosen in the oven — leave a clean border, and press the fold well before crimping.

Baking powder in an empanada dough?

Traditional for the sweet panadería style: slight lift keeps the crust short and tender instead of cracker-firm. Savory traditions often skip it; both are legitimate.

Can I freeze them?

Freeze filled-but-unbaked on trays then bag; bake from frozen plus five minutes, washing at minute ten. Baked ones stale fast — refresh five minutes in a hot oven.

How do I get that shiny panadería finish?

Egg wash before baking plus thin powdered-sugar glaze after = the classic double shine; simple syrup brushed on hot works too.
