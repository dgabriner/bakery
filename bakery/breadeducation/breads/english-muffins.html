Bread education / Breads / English muffins

No oven required

Bread that bakes in a frying pan.

English muffins are griddle bread: soft dough, cornmeal-dusted bottoms, slow patient heat — and the famous interior you tear open instead of slice.

The recipe card

Ten muffins, in grams

IngredientWeight
Bread flour · milk · butter softened · sugar · instant yeast · salt · semolina for dusting400 g · 240 g · 30 g · 15 g · 5 g · 8 g · handful
  1. Mix soft dough.
    Knead 6–8 min; tacky is correct.
  2. Bulk 1 h.
    Puffy, not doubled.
  3. Divide + semolina bath.
    90 g pieces, rounded, dusted all over.
  4. Proof on parchment.
    Flattened slightly; 45–60 min until airy.
  5. Griddle low & slow.
    Medium-low, 7–8 min/side; finish 200°F internal in a 300°F oven if dense-centered.
  6. Fork-split, never knife.
    Pry around the middle to keep the ragged walls.

Before you ask

Muffin questions

Why fork-split?

The fork follows gas tunnels and keeps ragged walls that hold butter — knife cuts them flat off.

Where do nooks come from?

Soft wet dough proofed high + gentle heat sets big bubbles before they collapse.

Burnt bottom, raw middle?

Griddle too hot. Medium-low, patience, thermometer finish.

Sourdough discard usable?

Up to half the starter as discard for tang; keep some yeast for lift (discard ideas).
