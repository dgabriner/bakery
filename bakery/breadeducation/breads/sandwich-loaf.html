Bread education / Breads / Sandwich loaf

Daily-driver loaf

Sourdough flavor, lunchbox manners.

This is the loaf that converts sourdough skeptics at breakfast: milk and butter soften the crumb, honey feeds gentle browning, and the tin keeps everything square enough for the toaster.

The recipe card

One tin, in grams

IngredientWeight
Bread flour · warm milk · soft butter · honey · active starter · salt450 g · 210 g · 30 g · 20 g · 100 g · 9 g
  1. Knead smooth.
    8–10 min; slightly softer than lean dough.
  2. Bulk to ~50%.
    Warm corner, covered.
  3. Shape cylinder.
    Flatten, roll tight, pinch seam; seam-down into buttered 23×13 cm tin.
  4. Proof to crown.
    60–90 min until dome rides ~2 cm above rim; poke springs slowly.
  5. Bake 375°F, 35–40 min.
    Internal 205°F. Cool 10 min in tin, then rack — fully.

Before you ask

Tin questions

Dense at the corners?

Under-proofed — dough never reached the edges. Crown height + slowpoke poke beat clocks.

Skip the honey?

Yes; add 15 g more milk. It drives tenderness/browning, not structure.

Toast slice thickness?

12–15 mm. Cooled loaves slice clean; day-one knives drag.

Levain or discard?

Active starter — one proof cycle needs real muscle. Discard goes to crackers.
