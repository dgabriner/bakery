Bread education / Breads / Ciabatta

The soap-bubble dough

Maximum holes. Zero shaping.

Ciabatta inverts everything loaf-shaped: eighty-plus percent hydration, no tension, no scoring — just folds, patience, and the discipline to not touch what should not be touched.

The recipe card

Two slippers, in grams

IngredientWeight
Bread flour · water · olive oil · salt · instant yeast450 g · 370 g (82%) · 25 g · 9 g · 3 g
  1. Mix, don't knead.
    Stir to shaggy batter; four fold-sets over two hours build all strength.
  2. Long cool bulk.
    To jiggly ~50% rise — or overnight in the fridge (retard).
  3. Pour, don't press.
    Onto a heavily floured linen; pat into a rectangle with floured fingertips.
  4. Divide in two.
    Scraper cuts; no rounding, no tightening — chaos is the crumb.
  5. Bake free on stone.
    230°C with steam rig (steam page), 22–26 min deep gold. Cool fully.

Before you ask

Slipper questions

Tight crumb instead of open?

Degassing somewhere: kneading post-hydration, pressing transfers, or tightened shaping. Folds yes, pressure never.

Easier hydration?

75% keeps the spirit and forgives hands; below that it's focaccia's cousin.

Need a biga?

No. Straight dough + long cool bulk is genuinely ciabatta.

Steam setup?

Rocks pan or inverted roaster from the steam page.
