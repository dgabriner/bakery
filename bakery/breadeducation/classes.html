Bake with a working bakery

Classes taught by people who bake every morning.

Sour Flour classes are hands-on and honest: real dough, real ovens, real timing. You leave having shaped, scored, and baked — not just watched.

Why it works

You learn faster with dough in your hands

Reading about fermentation is useful; feeling a dough pass the poke test while someone explains what they notice is faster. Every Sour Flour class pairs the ideas from this learning zone with bench time and a baker who answers questions while you work.

Small and hands-on

You shape your own loaves, score your own patterns, and take home what you bake — plus the notes to repeat it.

Taught from production

Our bakers shape hundreds of loaves a week. What they teach is what the bakery does daily, scaled honestly to a home kitchen.

Connected to the zone

Class handouts build on these free pages — fermentation cues, baker's math, shaping — so you can keep practicing after the class ends.

Before you come

Get ready in twenty minutes

  1. Skim the five factors.
    Time, temperature, hydration, ratio, food — the framework on the hub is the language our teachers use on the bench.
  2. Read the first-loaf sequence.
    The five steps to your first loaf mirror the class arc, so the day feels familiar instead of rushed.
  3. Bring your questions.
    A stuck bake from home is welcome material. Photos of crumb and a rough timeline turn any class into free troubleshooting.
