Bread education / Corporate workshops

Team building you can eat

Your team, our ovens, one unforgettable shift.

A Sour Flour corporate workshop puts your group on the bench of a working San Francisco bakery: real dough, production ovens, and bakers who explain what they notice while everyone works. Nobody watches a slideshow.

Why it works

Baking is team practice with instant feedback

A bake day runs on the same skills your team uses every quarter — divided roles, shared timing, and calm adjustment when something surprises you. The difference is that bread shows its results within hours, together.

Everyone works

No demos from the sidelines. Each person mixes, folds, shapes, scores, and loads their own loaves, guided by a baker who teaches while working.

Taught from production

Our teachers shape hundreds of loaves a week at markets and counters across the city. What they share is what the bakery actually does, scaled honestly to a group.

The result is shared

Ovens do not care about org charts. The table fills with loaves your team made together — the easiest debrief anyone has ever hosted.

The shape of a session

From first mix to warm loaves

  1. Tell us your crowd.
    Headcount, dates, and the vibe you want through the Sour Flour Hotline. Private sessions are arranged around production, not a fixed calendar.
  2. We shape the day.
    We build the session around your group — which doughs, which steps, how much bench time — and confirm the plan with you directly.
  3. Bench time.
    Your team works real dough with our bakers: mixing, folding, shaping, scoring. Questions are answered while hands are busy.
  4. Bake and take home.
    Loaves come out of production ovens, and everyone carries their own work home plus notes to repeat the bake.

Before you ask

Common questions from organizers

What happens at a corporate baking workshop?

A hands-on team session at our Mission District bakery where your group mixes, shapes, scores, and bakes real sourdough alongside bakers who do it every morning. Sessions are built around your group size and goals, then booked by arrangement through the Sour Flour Hotline.

Why is baking good team building?

Bread rewards exactly what teams practice at work: dividing tasks, timing shared steps, and adjusting when the dough disagrees with the plan. Everyone leaves with loaves they made together, which makes the day easy to remember and talk about afterward.

How do we book a workshop for our company?

Tell us your headcount, preferred dates, and what you want the day to feel like through the Sour Flour Hotline. We shape the session around your crowd and confirm details directly; there is no fixed public calendar for private sessions.

Where does the session take place?

At our production home inside historic La Victoria Bakery in San Francisco's Mission District, the same ovens that load wholesale mornings. Directions and arrival details are confirmed when we plan your session together.
