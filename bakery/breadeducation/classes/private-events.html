Why it works

The kind of party people describe for years

Anyone can book a room. Few celebrations end with every guest pulling their own baked loaf from a production oven — flour on their hands, scoring pattern of their own choosing, notes in their pocket.

All hands, all ages

Shaping and scoring welcome mixed groups: kids, grandparents, and everyone between work side by side, each at their own pace.

Real bakery, real ovens

Your session happens at the bakery behind San Francisco's morning bread — not a rented studio. Guests see where thousands of weekly loaves begin.

Guided, not lectured

A baker moves around the table answering questions as they arise, teaching the way production teaches: watch the dough, adjust, record, repeat.

The shape of an event

From hello to hot loaves

  1. Tell us the occasion.
    Birthday, family visit, or just a crew that wants to bake — send the date window and headcount through the Hotline.
  2. We shape it together.
    Ages, group size, and the feel you want decide which doughs and steps fill the session. We confirm the plan directly.
  3. Bake day.
    Guests mix, fold, shape, and score their own loaves with a baker guiding the table. Expect flour, laughter, and better bread than anyone expected on a first try.
  4. The takeaway.
    Everyone carries home their own baked loaf plus the notes to do it again — and the journal habit starts free at SF Baker.

Before you ask

Questions planners ask us

Do you host birthday parties and private celebrations?

Yes. Birthdays, family gatherings, and friend groups bake together by arrangement: you get the table, the dough, and a baker to guide everyone from first mix to hot loaf. Reach out through the Sour Flour Hotline to start.

Is a baking party good for kids?

Shaping and scoring dough suits mixed ages well, and every guest takes home what they made. Tell us the ages in your group through the Hotline so we shape the session and the recipes to fit.

How far ahead should we book?

Private sessions are scheduled around production, so the earlier you write, the easier your date is to hold. Send your crowd size and preferred window through the Sour Flour Hotline and we will confirm what works.

What should guests bring?

Nothing but clothes that can accept a little flour. We provide dough, tools, ovens, and guidance; guests leave with their own shaped, baked loaves plus notes to repeat the bake at home.
