How it starts

From first message to first delivery

  1. Say hello with numbers.
    Volume, product interests, and delivery window via the Sour Flour Hotline. Rough estimates are fine to start.
  2. Taste first.
    Meet the line at Noe Valley Farmer's Market or Civic Center, or arrange samples — the honest test is what sells out.
  3. Set the standing order.
    We agree products, counts, and a morning window, then hold that rhythm week after week. Adjustments follow the seasons and your menu.
  4. Grow at bread speed.
    Add items, shift days, or scale counts as you learn what moves. Standing relationships bend; they do not need to restart.

Before you ask

Questions buyers ask us

Who can open a wholesale account?

Cafes, restaurants, markets, and shops around San Francisco and the Bay Area that want fresh bread in the morning. Tell us your volume and delivery window through the Sour Flour Hotline and we will tell you what the ovens can do.

What products are available wholesale?

Our core line: classic San Francisco sourdough loaves, baguettes, bagels, pretzels, and the pan dulce family baked daily at La Victoria Bakery. Standing orders are set around your menu and adjusted seasonally.

How do deliveries work?

Bread is baked overnight and dropped on standing morning routes across the city — the same cadence that loads Friday's oven for Saturday's markets. Your account gets a regular window so staff always know when the crates land.

Can we visit or taste first?

Ask through the Hotline. You can meet the bread at Noe Valley Farmer's Market or Civic Center any market morning, and tasting what sells out fastest is the honest way to start a menu conversation.
